Learning doesn't always look like learning.

At The Kite, education is disguised as play, messy art, and outdoor exploration. Every moment of the day builds skills they'll carry for life.

How We Support Development

Click any area to learn more about our approach.

01

Cognitive

Building early logic, memory, and concentration through puzzles, sequencing games, and exploring how things work.

Through structured puzzles, sorting activities, and open-ended exploration, children develop critical thinking skills. They learn to predict outcomes, recognize patterns, and sustain focus over longer periods.

02

Communication

Expanding vocabulary and expression through daily story times, group discussions, and active listening.

Daily circle time, storytelling, and role-play activities help children expand their vocabulary and develop confidence in self-expression. We celebrate every attempt at communication.

03

Social

Learning to share spaces, cooperate on tasks, empathize with peers, and form meaningful friendships.

Collaborative projects, shared meals, and group play teach children essential social skills. They learn to negotiate, take turns, and understand that their actions affect others.

04

Emotional

Recognizing and naming feelings, building resilience, and developing a secure sense of self-worth.

We help children name their emotions, practice self-regulation techniques, and build resilience through supported risk-taking and celebrating small victories.

05

Physical

Strengthening gross motor skills (running, climbing) and fine motor skills (holding a pencil, threading beads).

Our outdoor spaces and movement activities build coordination, balance, and body awareness. Fine motor skills are developed through art, sensory play, and practical life activities.

06

Independence

Encouraging self-care routines, making personal choices, and taking responsibility for their environment.

Children are encouraged to manage their own belongings, pour their own water, choose their activities, and tidy up their workspace — building real-world confidence every day.

It looks like play. It's actually learning.
