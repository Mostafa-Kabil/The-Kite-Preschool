8:00 AM

Arrive

A warm start to the day. Teachers greet families, children put away their own belongings to build independence, and settle into self-directed morning play.

9:00 AM

Explore

Classroom activities open up. Children move freely between learning centers, engaging in puzzles, early reading, math concepts, and science experiments.

10:30 AM

Move

Outdoor and physical activities. Running, climbing, and cooperative games help children burn energy and develop gross motor skills.

11:30 AM

Create

Creative experiences in the art studio or music corner. Getting messy, expressing emotions, and turning imagination into reality.

1:00 PM

Connect

Group interaction and communication. Story time, circle time, and shared meals where children learn to listen, speak, and empathize.

3:00 PM

Go Home

A quiet transition. Gathering belongings, saying goodbye to friends and teachers, and heading home to rest and share stories.
