Daily Life
01 What does a day look like?
Our day balances structured group activities with generous blocks of self-directed play. Children arrive and settle in, move between creative and cognitive learning centers, spend significant time outdoors, and engage in social routines like shared meals and story time.

See our full day structure →
02 What should my child bring?
A comfortable change of clothes (learning can be messy!), a healthy packed lunch and snacks, a water bottle, and appropriate outerwear for outdoor play regardless of the weather.
Learning & Development
03 How do children learn?
Children learn through hands-on play. By providing a rich environment filled with engaging materials, children naturally develop cognitive, physical, and emotional skills while doing what they do best: playing.
04 What ages do you accept?
We welcome children starting from 1.5 years old in our Playgroup, up to 5 years old in our Senior classes.
Getting Started
05 How do you communicate with parents?
We believe in open, honest, and direct communication. You will speak with your child's teacher during drop-off and pick-up, receive regular informal updates about their developmental milestones, and attend scheduled parent-teacher meetings.
06 How do I arrange a visit?
The best way to understand our approach is to see it in action. You can book a visit through our contact form, or reach out to us directly via phone or WhatsApp.
