Our Story

More than a place to learn.

The Kite Preschool is an environment built entirely around the way young children naturally experience the world.

1:5 Teacher Ratio
100% Play-Based
Daily Outdoor Time

It starts with childhood.

The Kite exists because we believe early childhood shouldn't be rushed. It's a distinct and vital period of human development where play is the primary vehicle for learning.

We want children to experience the joy of deep curiosity, the happiness of untethered exploration, and the pride of growing independence. We don't just prepare them for school; we provide meaningful daily experiences that prepare them for life.

What we believe.

Hover over each value to discover what it means in practice.

Curiosity

Children should have the space and freedom to ask questions, test boundaries, and discover answers for themselves.

Independence

Children should have countless daily opportunities to try things themselves, from pouring water to resolving conflicts.

Communication

Children learn through relationships and interaction. We encourage them to express their feelings and ideas openly.

Problem Solving

Children learn best by trying, thinking, failing, and trying again. We provide the tools, not just the answers.

What makes The Kite different?

  • Small class sizes with a dedicated 1:5 teacher-to-student ratio
  • Dedicated outdoor learning spaces integrated into daily routines
  • A fully hands-on, play-based curriculum — no worksheets
  • A certified and passionate educational team
